# Launch decision log

Opened 2026-10-09 for plan package PR01 (Phase A) of
`docs/production-readiness-plan-2026-10-09.md`. Baseline commit
`b22cda1bfed8b8ddc0a84222b022c4e832ab5139`. Ledger rows that depend on a
decision reference it as `D-nn` in `docs/release/ledger.md`.

Nothing in this log has been decided. Every entry is **open** until the named
role records a decision with a date and the evidence or rationale behind it.
"Proposed starting position" is the plan's or the inventory's suggestion, not a
commitment. Commercial terms (prices, currencies, limits, trial length,
refunds) are deliberately left blank: this log does not invent them.

How to record a decision: change `Status` to `decided — <role>, <date>`, fill
`Decision` and `Rationale/evidence`, and update every ledger row that cites the
ID in the same pull request. A reversal adds a new dated line under the entry;
it never deletes the earlier one.

### Authority

1. The reference governs the visual system, layouts, component presentation
   and interaction patterns.
2. The repository governs data, security, permissions, integrations and
   business rules. No API route, `lib/server` module, migration or permission
   check was changed to fit the design. Where the design needs data the app
   does not have, the app renders what it has and the gap is recorded.
3. Prototype simulations and invented details (sample records, fake timers,
   `?state=` switches, sample photos, demo identities) are not requirements.
4. Material conflicts are recorded in "Reference vs repository decisions"
   below, not silently resolved.

## Direction

A cool paper canvas with white cards drawn by a 1px line at radius 12. No
decorative gradients, no blur, no card tints per client, queue or status.

- **One green accent.** The primary action, focus, the current nav item
  (tint plus a 3px left bar) and the selected row. Nothing else is green
  except the "ok" status.
- **Serif only for titles and the customer's words.** Page titles (`h1`,
  `.display`) and the review text use the serif stack. Everything else is
  the system sans. Figures, counts, IDs and timestamps are mono with tabular
  numerals.
- **Status is a word plus a tone.** A pill with a dot; never colour alone.
- **One dark action bar per screen** (below).
- 4px spacing scale; `gap-4` / `gap-6` between sections; controls 36px, 44px
  on coarse pointers.
- Icons: lucide-react at 16px (`size-4`), stroke 1.75 where settable.

What this retires from the warm-paper identity: the warm hue-85 neutrals, the
forest-green Today panel, Schibsted Grotesk, card surfaces without borders,
the 14/16/20 radius steps for cards and modals, and the caption bar. There is
no caption bar in this identity; the `caption-bar` utility and its
`--np-caption-bar*` roles remain in `app/globals.css` only until nothing
references them and must not be used by new work.

The toolbar is 56px. Workspace pages (the inbox) lock their height to the
viewport only where the window is at least 768×620 (`md:[@media(min-height:620px)]:`
variants); elsewhere the page scrolls and the action bar is sticky.

Route checkpoints: 320, 360, 390, 414, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 (and 1920 for the
shell and wide pages), plus just below, at and above each breakpoint a layout
uses. No page-wide horizontal scroll, no clipping, no hover-only actions,
44×44 primary touch targets on coarse pointers, long names wrap, dialogs and
sheets fit the viewport, sticky bars never cover content. Horizontal scroll
only inside a component that needs it (tab and chip rows, wide tables, an
overflowing chart plot), and made discoverable.

## The dark action bar

The one charcoal surface on a screen holds the action that reaches Google:
the inbox detail footer (reply status, the written reason, the primary
action), the listing editor footer, and the bulk bar. Toasts are charcoal
too; they are transient and do not count against the rule.

- At most one per screen. It is `ActionBar` (`components/ui/action-bar.tsx`)
  with `ActionBarMuted` for its secondary text.
- It wraps rather than clips. The reference clipped "Submit for approval" at
  1280px; the implementation was checked at 1024, 1180, 1280 and 1920.
- On phones and short windows it is `sticky bottom-0` with safe-area padding
  and must end below the last content, never over it.
- A disabled action on the bar carries its reason as text beside it.
- Saving locally is not publishing. In the inbox, "Save draft" stays in the
  composer, because moving it onto the bar would duplicate an accessible name
  tests rely on; the bar instead says "Save your draft before publishing."

## Status words

Status is always a word plus a tone. `lib/ui/status-tone.ts` holds the five
tones (`healthy`, `attention`, `at-risk`, `pending`, `neutral`), each with an
ink-on-tint pill pair that clears 4.5:1 in both themes and a solid dot.
`StatusPill` also accepts the reference tones `ok`, `warn`, `bad`, `info`,
`accent` and `outline`, and `dashed` and `plain` presentations.

Words that describe Google are only shown from evidence the app already has:

| State                             | Words                                            | Evidence required                                                       |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nothing published                 | "Nothing is live yet"                            | No accepted or published reply                                          |
| Accepted by Google, not confirmed | "Sent to Google — waiting for Google to confirm" | Publish status `accepted` / `publish_requested`; never drawn as success |
| Publishing                        | "Publishing…" (busy)                             | An operation in flight                                                  |
| Confirmed                         | "On Google now" / "Live on Google"               | Publish status `published` or verification evidence                     |
| Failed                            | the error, plus "Retry publish"                  | Publish status `failed`                                                 |
| No capability                     | "View-only access"                               | No edit, publish or request capability                                  |

`PublishSteps` renders only the states its caller passes; "sent" reads "Sent
to Google" and is never a success tone.

## Motion

- One curve, `--np-ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (the reference
  `--ease`), with `--np-ease-standard` for colour.
- Durations: 140ms for hover and colour (`--np-duration-fast`, the reference
  `--t-fast`), 260ms for movement (`--np-duration-standard`, `--t-move`),
  240ms for overlays, 360ms for entrances.
- Movement is limited to what the reference moves (the nav drawer and sheets
  slide, `--t-move`); colour changes use `--t-fast`. The inbox detail swaps
  in place on narrow screens. Nothing bounces; no springs; never
  `transition: all`.
- Hover fills run only for fine pointers (`hover-fine:`).
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every duration collapses to 0.01ms.
- The warm-paper page-load choreography (Today panel, staggered client
  cards and rows) is retired. `lib/motion/reveal.ts` and the `enter-stagger`
  utility remain for groups that already use them (the timeline and
  verification panel); content is never hidden by default.
- Test note: a hidden browser pane pauses `requestAnimationFrame`, so overlays
  can look stuck at opacity 0. Verify overlays with Playwright, and settle
  motion before axe samples a page.

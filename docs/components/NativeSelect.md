A styled native `<select>`.

Use where the OS picker is preferable — dense forms, mobile, or when JS-driven popovers are unwanted. Children are `NativeSelectOption` and `NativeSelectOptGroup`.

## Parts

Composed inside `<NativeSelect>`, each importable from `window.NabaPresence.*`:

- `NativeSelectOptGroup`
- `NativeSelectOption`

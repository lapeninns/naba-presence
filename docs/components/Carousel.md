Horizontally paged content.

Compose `CarouselContent` with `CarouselItem` children, plus `CarouselPrevious`/`CarouselNext`. Content behind a carousel is easily missed — do not hide essential information there.

## Parts

Composed inside `<Carousel>`, each importable from `window.NabaReview.*`:

- `CarouselContent`
- `CarouselItem`
- `CarouselNext`
- `CarouselPrevious`

A surface grouping related content.

Compose `CardHeader` (with `CardTitle`, `CardDescription`, and optionally `CardAction` for a top-right control), `CardContent`, and `CardFooter`. Do not nest cards inside cards — use `Separator` or `Item` for internal structure.

## Parts

Composed inside `<Card>`, each importable from `window.NabaReview.*`:

- `CardAction`
- `CardContent`
- `CardDescription`
- `CardFooter`
- `CardHeader`
- `CardTitle`

## How W8-04 and W4-03 fit together

W4-03's unconditional rewrite is what lets W8-04 happen. A `content_hash` skip (return early when the incoming payload equals the last one stored) would also skip the reply null-out in the common case. In that case the stale payload is the same as the pre-reply payload already stored, because the settle never updates `review.content_hash`. It would not cover a stale payload that differs for another reason (a reviewer edit, or variant C below). So both fixes are needed: the hash skip, plus guards on reply update time and on any in-flight `publish_attempt`.

## W8-04 interleavings (T = start of reconcile job S)

- T+0.0: S calls `googleReviews()`. Google has no reply yet.
- T+0.2: the publish intent commits: `review_reply` is `accepted` with body B, and the review is `publish_requested`.
- T+0.3: the reply PUT goes to Google and takes about 1–2 s.
- **Case A.** S's page transaction commits at T+0.5. The workflow CASE keeps `publish_requested`, because it is neither published nor rejected. The reply goes to body NULL, status `deleted`. The settle at T+1.5 then sets `published`, and the body stays NULL.
- **Case B.** The settle commits at T+1.5 and S commits at T+2.0. The workflow goes from `published` to `new` (`reviews.ts:283-289`), and the reply goes to body NULL, status `deleted`.
- **Variant C (new).** The operator edits an existing reply. A stale payload with the old reply overwrites `current_body` with the old text (`reviews.ts:361-370`), and the settle does not repair it.

All three heal at the next sync that sees the reply.

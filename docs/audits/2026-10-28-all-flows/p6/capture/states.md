# p6 capture states

Every state is shot at 390 light, 390 dark and 1280 light (390×844 touch/mobile; 1280×800).

- **Config A:** billing off, MFA off.
- **Config B:** `BILLING_ENABLED=true` and `MFA_ENABLED=true` on the app process.
- **Both configs:** the fixture org has `privacy_settings` and `workspace_mode_change` on. See README.md.

No interaction presses a final confirm.
